Who we are

A Cairo fabric house, built on quality and trust

Founded in 2006 by Shady Anwar, Al Azhar Tex has grown into one of Egypt's trusted names in wholesale women's fabrics. What began as a small family trading house is today a full-scale textile supplier serving boutiques, ateliers, ready-to-wear brands and manufacturers.

Our specialisation is the fabric of women's fashion — lustrous silks, airy georgettes, crisp satins and everyday wearables in the weights our customers ask for by name: Silk 180, Warsaw L, Pirlanta, Rotana 150, Rotana 180, Lexus 150 and Lexus 180. Every bolt is inspected before it reaches a customer.

  • Wholesale-only model — honest bulk prices without retail mark-up
  • Quality inspection on every bolt before dispatch
  • Reliable restocking for repeat collections
  • Delivery across Egypt, with careful packing for exports

Why Al Azhar Tex

Built for wholesale buyers

We sell to the trade — boutiques, ateliers, manufacturers and exporters — and we run every part of the business around bulk orders.

How ordering works

From first call to delivery, in four steps

  1. 1

    Request the catalogue

    Send us a message with the fabrics you're interested in. We reply with weights, colour charts and trade prices.

  2. 2

    Approve samples

    Order swatches or small pieces to check hand-feel, drape and shade before committing volume.

  3. 3

    Place your bulk order

    Confirm quantities and colours. We reserve stock and confirm your delivery window.

  4. 4

    Pickup or delivery

    Collect from our Cairo facility or have it delivered — across Egypt, or packed for export.
